Objective: minimize the total added system dispatch cost (equivalently: avoid

1. Deferred compute must be served within 24 hours (the environment

2. 0 <= battery state of charge <= capacity; |battery power| <= limit
3. flex_serve_mw >= 0; serving beyond arrivals draws down the backlog

## Recommended Improvement: Opportunistic Battery Discharge for Price Arbitrage

### The Problem
The policy only discharges battery defensively (when backlog is urgent). It misses **proactive arbitrage**: discharge battery during high-price periods to offset firm load, even without immediate backlog deadline pressure.
