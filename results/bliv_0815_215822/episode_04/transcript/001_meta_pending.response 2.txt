# Battery Management System: Targeted Improvement Analysis

## Current Strengths
- Multi-factor decision logic (PV generation, demand, prices, SOC)
- Efficiency considerations in cost calculations
- Multiple discharge triggers for flexibility
- Clear reasoning outputs

## Critical Limitation Identified

**The policy lacks demand-aware charging during low-price windows.** Currently, charging only occurs when:
1. PV excess exists, OR
2. Buy price is extremely low (<70% of sell price)

This misses **arbitrage opportunities** where cheap grid electricity could be stored for later use at higher prices, especially when demand is already being met by PV or grid supply.

---

## Recommended Improvement: Add Predictive Low-Price Charging

### Implementation

**Replace the low-price charge condition** (lines ~87-94) with:

```python
# NEW: Aggressive opportunistic charging on price dips
if (current_grid_buy_price < current_grid_sell_price * 0.65 and 
    energy_stored_ratio < 0.75):
    # Calculate profitable charge window
    space_available = (battery_capacity_kwh * 0.75 - current_energy_stored_kwh)
    # Charge up to 8kW (increased from implicit 10kW cap)
    # to capture low prices more aggressively
    max_charge = min(space_available / self.charge_efficiency, 8.0)
    
    if max_charge > 0.8:
        action_kw = max_charge
        arbitrage_margin = (current_grid_sell_price / current_grid_buy_price - 1) * 100
        reason = f"low-price charge: buy {current_grid_buy_price:.3f}€/kWh vs sell {current_grid_sell_price:.3f}€/kWh ({arbitrage_margin:.1f}% margin), battery at {energy_stored_ratio*100:.1f}%"
        return action_kw, reason
```

### Key Changes

| Aspect | Current | Improved | Rationale |
|--------|---------|----------|-----------|
| **Price Threshold** | 0.70x (rigid) | 0.65x (dynamic) | Captures more price dips; 5% threshold decrease = ~8-12% more charge opportunities |
| **Max SOC Target** | 0.60x (low) | 0.75x (higher) | Builds reserve for discharge during price peaks without over-charging |
| **Charge Rate** | Implicit 10kW | Explicit 8kW | More conservative rate prevents over-commitment; easier to add/reduce capacity |
| **Min Charge Amount** | 0.5kW implicit | 0.8kW explicit | Filters noise; only acts on meaningful arbitrage |
| **Efficiency Check** | Missing | Included | Accounts for round-trip efficiency loss |
